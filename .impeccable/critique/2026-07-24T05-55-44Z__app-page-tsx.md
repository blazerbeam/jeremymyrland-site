## Anti-Patterns Verdict

Not obviously AI-generated — the copy voice, real metrics, and headshot carry personality. But three mechanical tells surfaced:

- **Side-stripe borders** (detector): `border-l-2` on testimonials (testimonials-section.tsx:77) and `border-l-4` on writing post (writing/[slug]/page.tsx:112). The Building-section blockquotes use `border-l-2 border-primary` too — legitimate as a real quote, borderline as a card accent.
- **Bounce easing** (detector): `animate-bounce` scroll indicator (hero-section.tsx:106). Reads as dated; should be a smooth ease.
- **Identical card grids**: Work, Building, Testimonials, Education all render as the same bordered rounded card. Uniform rhythm = template feel.

## Overall Impression

Strong, credible, and on-brand. The single biggest opportunity is **depth**: background (#0F1117), card (#1A1D27), and border (#2A2D3A) all sit in one narrow dark band, so the page reads as a flat dark slab. That is what "too dark" actually is here — a value-separation problem, not a contrast failure.

## What's Working

- **Copy voice.** Direct, systems-first, no fluff. Rare for a portfolio.
- **Evidence over adjectives.** Real numbers, named companies, case-study modals.
- **Calm editorial layout.** Serif display + generous spacing.

## Priority Issues

- **[P1] Flat value ladder ("too dark").** bg/card/border within ~one dark band → no depth. Fix: raise bg lightness a touch, open the gap between bg and card, lift muted text and borders. Command: `/impeccable colorize` (or `quieter`/`polish`).
- **[P1] Hero/Work title contradiction.** Hero: "Staff PM and Director of Product." Work: "Principal PM, HR Global Technology." Pick one. Command: `/impeccable clarify`.
- **[P2] Muted body contrast thin.** #8A8FA8 on bg ≈5.9:1, on card ≈5.2:1 — passes AA but not generous for long summaries. Lifting it also helps the darkness. Command: `/impeccable colorize`.
- **[P2] Identical card grids.** Vary card treatment section-to-section. Command: `/impeccable layout`.
- **[P3] Bounce scroll cue + side-stripe borders.** Swap for smooth ease; convert side stripes to full borders / tints. Command: `/impeccable polish`.

## Minor Observations

- Headshot loads from a Vercel blob URL, not `public/` + `next/image` — no width/height, LCP + layout-shift cost.
- Nav has no scroll-spy active state; no skip-to-content link.
- Case-study modal keyboard behavior (Esc, focus trap, return focus) should be verified.

## Questions to Consider

- If the site's thesis is "I ship real systems," does one more value-step of depth make it feel more crafted without losing the calm?
- Should Roost, as the first shipped commercial product, get a visually distinct treatment rather than a fourth identical card?
