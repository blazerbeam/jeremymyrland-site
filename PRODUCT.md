# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Hiring managers, recruiters, and senior tech leaders evaluating Jeremy for Staff /
Principal / Director of Product roles — plus peers, founders, and parents who arrive
through one of his shipped products (Roost, bethere.community, pairwise) or his writing.

Context of use: skimming, often on a phone, with a few seconds of patience. The implicit
question they arrive with is "is this person credible, and is he interesting enough to
keep reading / reach out?" They are not looking for a resume dump; they are pattern-matching
for signal — scope, ownership, and evidence that he actually ships.

## Product Purpose

A personal site that is itself a proof point. The thesis: Jeremy takes broken, fragmented
systems and makes them work at the system level — across a 15-year PM career (Nike, Apple,
Workday, Wayfair) and now as a solo founder shipping real products without a CS background.
The site exists to establish credibility fast, show the range from enterprise scope to
indie execution, and open a conversation (contact / LinkedIn). Success = a qualified reader
leaves convinced and takes one action (reads a post, opens a product, reaches out).

## Brand Personality

Operator, not influencer. Direct, systems-first, quietly confident. Three words:
**grounded, precise, builder.** The voice explains how things actually work and what to do
next — short sentences, concrete outcomes, opinion when it earns its place. Emotional goal:
earned trust. The reader should feel they are hearing from someone who has done the work,
not someone performing it.

## Anti-references

- Generic SaaS / template portfolio (icon + heading + paragraph cards repeated down the page).
- Inspirational-guru / thought-leader tone ("I'm passionate about leveraging synergies").
- AI-slop tells: gradient text, tracked-uppercase eyebrows on every section, side-stripe
  accent borders, the hero-metric template used decoratively, corporate hedging.
- Over-designed agency showreel that buries the substance under motion and effects.

## Design Principles

1. **Practice what you preach.** The site is a shipped product; its craft is itself evidence.
   No detail that a careful builder would leave broken.
2. **Show, don't tell.** Lead with concrete outcomes, real artifacts, and named products
   over adjectives. Numbers and links do the persuading.
3. **Systems over features.** Frame each role and project around the underlying problem and
   the system-level fix, not a feature list.
4. **Editorial restraint.** Magazine calm, generous space, one confident accent. Let the
   writing carry weight; resist decorative clutter.
5. **Honest confidence.** State scope plainly without inflation or false modesty. The tone
   is an experienced operator, not a pitch.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.1 AA. Body text ≥4.5:1, large/secondary text ≥3:1 — the muted-gray-on-dark
body copy is the area most at risk and must be verified, not assumed. Full keyboard
navigation with visible focus, meaningful alt text on the headshot and any real artifacts,
and a `prefers-reduced-motion` alternative for every scroll/entrance animation.
